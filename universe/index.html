Universe

Nine spray-paint paintings of planets and the space around them. Scroll, and the ship flies you from one to the next.

Exploring graffiti painting as a medium, inspired by space and planets. The goal is to explore multiple techniques within the graffiti medium while representing the infinite and mysterious possibilities of space.
